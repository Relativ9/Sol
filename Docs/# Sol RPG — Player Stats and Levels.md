Player Stats and Levels
Players start their Sol campaign at level 1 and level up by gaining XP which they get from killing enemies, completing quests, crafting objects, or harvesting resources. The max player level is 100.

Each time a player levels up they get 1 talent point to assign and 3 stat points.

Stats:



### 1. Brawn

_The Stat of Physical Force._

- **Offense:** Increases **Melee Damage**.
- **Defense:** Reduces **Movement Speed Penalty** from wearing armor.
- **Utility:** Increases **Inventory Capacity**.
- **Social:** Increases **Intimidation** success chance.
- **Appeals to:** Warriors (Damage), Battlemages (Heavy Armor mobility), Hoarders.

### 2. Finesse

_The Stat of Agility and Movement._

- **Offense:** Increases **Archery Damage**.
- **Movement:** Increases **Sprint Speed**.
- **Utility:** Reduces **Fall Damage**. Improves **Stealth**. Improves **Lock-picking**.
- **Social:** Increases **Deception** success chance.
- **Appeals to:** Archers (Damage), Rogues (Stealth), Skirmishers (Speed).

### 3. Insight

_The Stat of Knowledge and Anatomy._

- **Offense:** Increases **Spell Power**.
- **Combat:** Increases **Critical Damage Multiplier** (How hard you hit _when_ you crit).
- **Defense:** Increases **Magic Resistance**.
- **Utility:** Increases **Potion, Poison, and Enchantment Potency**. Improves **Mastercraft Chance**.
- **Social:** Increases **Knowledge** check success chance.
- **Appeals to:** Mages (Power), Warriors (Magic Defense & Harder Crits), Alchemists (Potions).

### 4. Focus

_The Stat of Perception and Precision._

- **Combat:** Increases **Global Critical Hit Chance** (Spells, Melee, Arrows).
- **Combat:** Increases **Projectile Range** (Arrows & Spells).
- **Defense:** Increases the **Timing Window** for reactions (Parry, Perfect Block, Spell Ward).
- **Utility:** Highlights **Hidden Loot, Secrets, and Ingredients**.
- **Appeals to:** Snipers (Range/Crit), Duelists (Parry), Explorers (Loot).

### 5. Vigor

_The Stat of Fitness and Metabolism._

- **Survival:** Increases **Maximum Health**.
- **Recovery:** Increases **Energy Regeneration Rate**.
- **Defense:** Reduces **Environmental Damage** (Heat, Cold, Poison) and **Trap Damage**.
- **Appeals to:** **Everyone.** This is the "Don't Die" stat. It governs how fast you get your stamina back to keep fighting.

### 6. Willpower

_The Stat of Mental Discipline._

- **Resource:** Increases **Maximum Energy**.
- **Efficiency:** Reduces **Global Cooldown** (GCD).
- **Efficiency:** Reduces the energy cost of blocking, holding your breath, drawing an arrow, and channeling spells.
- **Defense:** Reduces the **Spell Cost Penalty** from Armor.
- **Social:** Increases **Charm** success chance.
- **Appeals to:** Casters (Mana Pool), Tanks (Block Cost), Hybrid Battlemages (Casting in Armor).
